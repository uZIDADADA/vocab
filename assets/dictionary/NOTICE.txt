This is a community-compiled dictionary, not an Oxford, Collins or Cambridge
licensed dictionary. Upstream frequency and exam labels are selection metadata,
not an attribution of the definitions to those publishers. Coverage and
definitions may be incomplete, dated or incorrect; no AI translations are used.
Chinese definitions are independent of WordNet's English senses, not aligned
translations of those senses. Personal definitions are shown separately.

Vocab selects BNC/FRQ top-20,000 or exam/oxford/collins-tagged entries with Han
definitions and English headwords. Original Chinese text is preserved; literal
newline escapes are decoded. Search keys are lowercased. Reverse lookup indexes
Han sequences (excluding bracketed labels); exact matches precede prefix matches.
The database excludes audio, English definitions and other redundant fields.
Reproduce using tool/build_bilingual_dictionary.py; source and asset checksums,
counts and selection rules are recorded in ecdict-core-v1.manifest.json.
